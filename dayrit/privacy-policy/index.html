Privacy Policy

Dayrit

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Dayrit ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in using Apple or Google login via Firebase Authentication, we collect the following:

  • Email address
  • Display name (as provided by your login provider)
  • User ID (unique identifier assigned by Firebase)

Habit Data: We store the following data in Firebase Firestore to provide our service:

  • Habit settings (name, icon, repeat days, reminder time, etc.)
  • Habit completion records (per-date completion status or counts)

Locally Stored Data: The following data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • App settings and preferences (via SharedPreferences)
  • Notification settings
  • Home screen widget display data (via shared local storage)
  • Premium purchase status cache (actual purchase verification is handled by Apple/Google)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the habit tracking service
  • To sync your habit data across devices
  • To manage your account and premium purchase
  • To display relevant advertisements (for non-premium users)
  • To improve our app and user experience

4. Advertising

Dayrit uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements to non-premium users. AdMob may collect and use certain data for ad personalization, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Usage data
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)

You can learn more about Google's advertising data practices at Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS, we request tracking permission via App Tracking Transparency; if you decline, only non-personalized ads will be shown.

Premium users are not shown any advertisements and no advertising-related data is collected for them.

5. In-App Purchase

Dayrit offers a one-time premium purchase through Apple's App Store and Google Play Store. All payment processing is handled by Apple or Google respectively. We do not collect or store your payment information; we only receive purchase confirmation.

6. Notifications

Dayrit uses local notifications only to send habit reminders. We do not use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) or any push notification service. Notification data is processed entirely on your device. You can enable or disable notifications at any time through the app settings or your device settings.

7. Home Screen Widget

Dayrit provides home screen widgets to display your habit data. Widget data is shared between the main app and the widget extension using shared local storage on your device. This data remains local and is not transmitted to any external servers.

8. Data Storage and Security

Cloud Storage: Your account and habit data are stored in Firebase Firestore, which provides industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest.

Local Storage: App settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

9. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through the app settings. When you delete your account:

  • All your habit data stored in Firestore will be permanently deleted
  • Your Firebase Authentication account will be removed
  • Locally stored data will be cleared
  • This action is irreversible

10. Children's Privacy

Dayrit is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

11. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): For user sign-in. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Firestore (Google): For cloud data storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics (Google): For usage analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): For crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): For remote app configuration. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): For displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
